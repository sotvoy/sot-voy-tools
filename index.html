WHY THIS EXISTS

The internet has enough tools that make simple things feel complicated.

SOT VOY Tools is being built in the opposite direction: focused pages, clear controls, useful explanations and results you can understand immediately.

As the library grows, the standard stays the same. More tools should mean more usefulness—not more clutter.

FAQ

Good questions deserve straight answers.

Is SOT VOY Tools free to use?+

The tools are being built to be freely accessible. Some parts of the platform may eventually be supported by advertising, but the core experience is designed around useful free tools.

Do I need an account?+

No account should be necessary for ordinary browser tools. We want a quick calculation or conversion to stay a quick calculation or conversion.

Does SOT VOY upload my data?+

For tools that can safely process information in the browser, our goal is to keep that processing on your device. Each tool will explain its data behavior clearly.

Will more tools be added?+

Yes. The platform is being designed as an expandable library, with the long-term goal of covering hundreds of genuinely useful everyday and developer tasks.
