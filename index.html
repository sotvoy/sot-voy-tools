Useful beats impressive.

There are thousands of “free tools” online. Many are covered in ads, buried in clutter, or make a ten-second task feel complicated.

SOT VOY Tools follows a simpler idea: open it, understand it, get the result, leave.

01

Fast enough to forget.

Lightweight pages and browser-first processing where it makes sense.

02

Clear about your data.

Explain what happens instead of making vague privacy promises.

03

Designed for small screens.

Every interaction has to work comfortably on the phone you already have.

Nothing complicated.

Do I need an account? +

No. The core tools are designed to work without an account.

Are the tools free? +

The core library is intended to remain free to use. Advertising may support hosting and development.

Will there be more tools? +

Yes. The architecture is intended to grow well beyond the initial 20+ tools.

Does it work on phones? +

Yes. Mobile is a first-class target.
